what drinks can i seal?

anything cold you already make: matcha, smoothies, cold brew, juice, protein shakes, lemonade. if you serve it cold, you can seal it.

how fast does it seal?

about 3 seconds a can. one button, no training.

what size are the cans?

330 ml tall clear cans with aluminum lids. the clear body is the point - people photograph the drink.

what comes in each package?

signature: the machine, cans printed with your logo, lids, and label design. basic: the machine plus blank cans and lids. machine only: just the sealer, in white or black.

how does the branding work?

you upload your design in the cart and we check it against the 2x2 inch sticker before anything prints. we print the labels and apply every one, so the cans arrive ready to fill. the signature package also includes designing the label with you. basic comes blank if you'd rather label them yourself later.

how long do sealed drinks last?

the seal keeps drinks fresh and spill-proof - it doesn't preserve them. treat a sealed can like the drink inside: keep it refrigerated and follow your kitchen's usual shelf life.

will it fit my counter?

yes. it's a countertop machine that plugs into a normal outlet. no plumbing, no special power.

can i seal hot drinks?

the machine is designed for cold drinks. if you serve it over ice, you can seal it.

where do i get more cans?

refill packs on the shop page. reorder any time, delivered to your door.

what if something breaks?

if your machine arrives damaged or stops working in your first 30 days, we replace it. unopened orders can be returned within 14 days. cans with your branding can be refunded any time before your labels go to print, and if a print doesn't match the proof you approved we reprint it free.

how do i pay?

card checkout by stripe, or e-transfer and save 3% on everything.

how do i get it?

delivery takes 5-7 business days. for cans with your branding that clock starts once we approve your design. delivery and setup are free in the gta; outside it we ship anywhere in canada and confirm shipping cost within 1 business day.
