the product

What drinks can I seal?

Anything cold you already make: matcha, smoothies, cold brew, juice, protein shakes, lemonade. If you serve it cold, you can seal it.

How fast does it seal?

About three seconds a can. One button, no training.

What size are the cans?

330 ml tall clear cans with aluminum lids. The clear body is the point. People photograph the drink.

What comes in each package?

Signature: the machine, 200 cans with your label applied, lids, and label design. Basic: the machine plus 200 blank cans and lids. Machine only: just the sealer, in white or black.

Will it fit my counter?

Yes. It's a countertop machine about the size of a blender that plugs into a normal outlet. No plumbing, no special power.

Can I seal hot drinks?

The machine is made for cold drinks. If you serve it over ice, you can seal it.

How long do sealed drinks last?

The seal keeps drinks fresh and spill-proof. It doesn't preserve them. Treat a sealed can like the drink inside: keep it cold and follow your kitchen's usual shelf life.

your design

How does the branding work?

On the product page you tell us how to get your design: upload a logo, point us at your Instagram or website, or have us draw it. We check it against the 2×2 inch sticker and send you a proof. Once you approve it, we print the labels and apply every one, so the cans arrive ready to fill.

Do you design my label?

Yes. It's included in the signature package. With a branded can pack it's a paid add-on, added to your cart automatically when you pick "design it for me".

What file should I send?

PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF under 4 MB. Square art at 600px or more prints sharpest. No file? Give us your Instagram handle and we'll take it from there.
